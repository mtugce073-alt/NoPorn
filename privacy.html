This Privacy Policy explains how NoPorn (the "App", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses and protects information when you use our mobile application and this support website.

NoPorn is a digital habit-tracking app designed to help users build healthier routines, track their streaks and monitor their progress over time. The App is intended for informational and organizational purposes only and does not display, store or distribute any sexual content.

2. Information We Collect

We aim to collect and store only the minimum information necessary to operate and improve the App.

  • Subscription-related information: information about your purchase status (for example, whether you are on an active subscription or trial) as provided by the Apple App Store and any subscription management service we use (such as RevenueCat).

When you use the App, certain technical information may be collected automatically, for example:

  • Device model, operating system version and language settings.

  • Crash logs and performance data to help us fix issues.

This information is generally aggregated and does not directly identify you as an individual.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use the collected information to:

  • Provide the core features of the App (habit and streak tracking).

The App is intended for use by adults and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided information to us, please contact us and we will delete it.
